Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Refunds depend on what you bought and how far along we are. The rules below apply unless your written quote says something different. For a booked trip, the quote always wins.

Ready blueprint

  • If you have paid and we have not sent the PDF to your WhatsApp within 24 hours, message us and we will send it or refund you in full.
  • Once the PDF has been sent it cannot be refunded, because it is a digital product, unless the file is damaged or clearly not the route you bought. In that case we replace it or refund you.

Custom plan

  • Cancel before we start building your plan: full refund.
  • After we have started: the fee is not refundable, because the work is done. Your free revision is there to fix anything that does not fit your brief.
  • If we cannot deliver a plan that fits your brief: we refund the fee.

Group packages and booked trips

When we book hotels, vehicles or activities for you, those providers set their own cancellation rules. Your written quote lists the cancellation schedule for each booking before you pay the advance.

If you cancel, we refund the money we recover from providers plus any part of your payment that has not been spent, after deducting charges that providers keep and any payment-gateway fee that is not returned to us. We show you the numbers behind every deduction.

If we cancel or cannot deliver

If we have to cancel a booked trip or part of it, for example because of unsafe conditions or a provider failing, we will offer an alternative first. If you prefer a refund, we return everything you paid that has not been spent and is recoverable, and we show you proof of what is not recoverable.

Weather and events outside our control

Landslides, floods, strikes, closures and similar events can change a trip. We will rebook or adjust where we can. Costs already spent that cannot be recovered cannot be refunded, but we pass on every refund we receive from providers.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. They are usually credited within 5 to 7 working days after we process them, though your bank can take longer.
