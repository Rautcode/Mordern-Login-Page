Questions

Everything people ask before booking

Do you book trains and flights, or just give us the exact train/flight numbers?

The Blueprint and Custom Plan tiers give you exact train and flight numbers, timings and connections so you can book on IRCTC or your airline. With a Full Trip Execution package we handle the bookings; the scope is confirmed in your written quote.

How fast will I receive my custom itinerary after submitting the form?

Within 24 hours of confirmed details, with one free revision included. Very large or multi-city groups may need a quick call first, and we tell you upfront.

Can you organise transport and hotels for a large group?

Yes. We arrange transport and group-friendly stays with local partners, and the per-person split is shown before you commit. Our route plans come from our own group trips of 14 or more people.

How do payments and advances work for full packages?

Full packages run on a 50% advance to lock bookings. The balance schedule and every line item are written in your quote before you pay.

Do you cover North India destinations like Manali, Kasol, and Rishikesh?

Yes, on request. Choose Himachal/Uttarakhand in the planner and we will tell you honestly what we can plan and arrange for your dates.

How much does a trip cost?

Every trip is quoted around your budget and requirements, so there is no fixed price list. Tell us your dates, who is travelling and your budget in the planner and we send a firm written quote within 24 hours.

What do I get in the free preview versus the paid Custom Plan?

The free preview shows Day 1 of your itinerary in full so you can judge the quality. The paid Custom Plan unlocks every day, the food map and the priority cut-lists.
