Questions

Everything people ask before booking.

Do you book trains and flights, or just give us the exact train/flight numbers?

The Blueprint and Custom Plan tiers give you exact train and flight numbers, timings and connections so you can book on IRCTC or your airline. With a Full Trip Execution package we handle the bookings; the scope is confirmed in your written quote.

How fast will I receive my custom itinerary after submitting the form?

Within 24 hours of confirmed details, with one free revision included. Very large or multi-city groups may need a quick call first, and we tell you upfront.

Can you organise a Tempo Traveller and hotels for a 10–16 person group from Pune?

Yes. Pune and PCMC pickups, Tempo Travellers and group-friendly stays are our core use case, with the per-person split shown before you commit.

How do payments and advances work for full packages?

Full packages run on a 50% advance to lock bookings. The balance schedule and every line item are itemised in your quote, with no hidden agency markups.

Do you cover North India destinations like Manali, Kasol, and Rishikesh?

Yes. We work with vetted local ground teams across Himachal, Uttarakhand and Rajasthan. Choose “Himachal/Uttarakhand” in the planner and we'll route it.

How much does a group trip from Pune cost?

Group packages start from ₹8,499 per person for Goa, ₹9,999 for Kerala, ₹10,499 for Gokarna–Hampi and ₹11,499 for Rajasthan. Final prices depend on dates, group size and stay type, and are confirmed in your quote.

What do I get in the free preview versus the paid Custom Plan?

The free preview shows Day 1 of your itinerary in full so you can judge the quality. The ₹2,499 Custom Plan unlocks every day, the food map and the priority cut-lists.
