Do you book trains and flights, or just give us the exact train/flight numbers?

Blueprint and Custom Plan tiers give you exact train/flight numbers, timings and connections so you can book on IRCTC or your airline of choice. With a Full Trip Execution package, we handle the bookings for you; the scope is confirmed in your written quote.

How fast will I receive my custom itinerary after submitting the form?

Within 24 hours of your confirmed details, with one free revision included. Very large or multi-city groups may need a quick call first, and we'll tell you upfront.

Can you organize a Tempo Traveller and hotels for a 10–16 person group from Pune?

Yes. Pune/PCMC pickups, Tempo Travellers and group-friendly stays are our core use case, with the per-person split shown before you commit.

How do payments and advances work for full packages?

Full packages run on a 50% advance to lock bookings, with the balance due before departure. Everything is itemised in your quote, with no hidden agency markups.

Do you cover North India destinations like Manali, Kasol, and Rishikesh?

Yes. We work with vetted local ground teams across Himachal and Uttarakhand, as well as Rajasthan. Pick “Himachal/Uttarakhand” in the planner and we'll route it.
