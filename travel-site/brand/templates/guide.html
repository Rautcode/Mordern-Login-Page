01 Logo

One mark, used simply

Primary, on paper or white
Reversed, on forest green or dark photos
White, on orange or over a darkened photo
One colour: stamps, forms, embossing
Icon: profile pictures, favicon, app tile
Clear space: keep one G-height free on every side

Do

  • Use the files in brand/ as they are.
  • Minimum size: logo 30 mm or 120 px wide, icon 16 px.
  • Put it on flat colour, or on a photo with a dark overlay.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Recolour it outside the palette, or retype the name in another font.
  • Place it on a busy photo with no overlay.

Photos

  • Use your own trip photos. No stock beaches, no photos from other sites.
  • Taped-polaroid style with a handwritten caption.
  • Ask the people in a photo before you post it, and keep their yes in writing.
  • Straight, natural light; crop to the people or the place.

03 Voice and rules

Plain, specific, honest

How we sound

  • Friendly and direct, like a well-organised friend.
  • Concrete: times, places, what is included, what is not.
  • Short sentences. No hype words.

Rules we keep

  • No invented numbers, ratings or traveller counts.
  • No prices unless they are in a written quote.
  • No "verified", "vetted" or "safe" unless we can show how.
  • Reviews and photos only with the traveller's written yes.
  • No fake urgency: only say "limited seats" when it is true.
