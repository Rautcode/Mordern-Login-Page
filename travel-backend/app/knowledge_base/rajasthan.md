## Itinerary as designed
- **Day 1 (Sat): Depart Mumbai.** Assemble at Mumbai Central 17:30 with luggage; board 12955 around 19:05. Overnight on the train.
- **Day 2 (Sun): Jaipur, Amer by evening light.** Arrive Jaipur Jn 12:00 (a midday arrival buys more daylight). Hotel/hostel check-in and lunch. 14:30–16:30 Amer Fort: courtyards and the Sheesh Mahal (operator states it is open until 21:00, so no rush). 19:30 Hawa Mahal illuminated facade: exterior photo stop, no entry needed. From 20:30 street-food crawl (pyaaz kachori at Rawat Mishtan Bhandar) and rooftop dinner in C-Scheme.
- **Day 3 (Mon): Pink City by day, desert train by night.** 09:00–12:00 City Palace and Jantar Mantar. 12:30–14:30 Nahargarh Fort, lunch with a skyline view (Padao restaurant, inside the fort). 15:30 leave for Jaipur Jn with luggage: winding road down plus city traffic, so leave with margin. 16:25 board 12468. Overnight on the train.
- **Day 4 (Tue): Jaisalmer, living fort and the ghost village.** 05:05 arrive Jaisalmer; a second, separate 12-seater AC Tempo Traveller (Jaisalmer/Jodhpur leg) meets the group. 06:00–09:30 check in, freshen up, breakfast. 10:00–13:30 Jaisalmer Fort ramparts and Patwon ki Haveli. 14:30 drive to the desert, stopping at Kuldhara (abandoned village). 15:30 check in at a Sam Sand Dunes desert camp. 16:30–19:00 4x4 dune bashing and quad biking at sunset. From 20:00 folk music, campfire, dinner, stargazing.
- **Day 5 (Wed): Desert highway, zip-line, departure.** 08:00 breakfast and camp checkout (moved earlier than a 09:30 start to protect the afternoon). 08:30 depart for Jodhpur via NH125: a real 280–285 km, 4.5–6 hours, so treat 14:30 as the earliest realistic arrival, not the guaranteed one. On arrival (about 14:30–15:30) Flying Fox zip-lining at Mehrangarh Fort: book the earliest available slot as soon as the ETA firms up. About 17:00–17:30 Jaswant Thada, a quick visit right after the zip-line. 19:00–20:30 dinner at Stepwell Square (Toorji Ka Jhalra). 20:30 drop at Jodhpur Jn with a real buffer. 22:00 board 20495.
- **Day 6 (Thu): On the rails.** Arrive Pune Jn 16:40 (18h40 journey). Whoever is picking the group up should go to Pune Jn.

## Hard deadlines (operator-verified)
- **Flying Fox at Mehrangarh:** operates 09:30–17:00 only. The tour takes 90–120 minutes and a slot is capped at 12 people, so a group of 10 may need to split. This is the one appointment on the trip that cannot slide.
- **Jaswant Thada:** closes between 17:00 and 18:00.
- **Trains:** 12955 departs 19:05; 12468 departs 16:25; 20495 departs 22:00.
- **Jodhpur by road:** do not plan an arrival before 14:30 after an 08:30 departure from Sam.
