## Itinerary as designed
- **Day 1 (Fri): Kochi Airport → Munnar.** 03:30 land Kochi; driver meets at arrivals with a name board; 45 minutes to freshen up. 04:15 depart for Munnar (everyone sleeps in the Tempo Traveller). 06:30 breakfast at a highway dhaba in Kothamangalam (puttu + kadala curry, idiyappam + egg curry, hot chai; about Rs. 80 per person). 08:30 Cheeyappara Waterfalls on NH85 (30 min; free; slippery rocks, stay away from the edge). 09:30 reach Munnar and check in at Sea Grace Cottage; rest an hour. 11:00 Attukad Waterfalls (9 km from town; narrow road, the Tempo Traveller parks at the base, 5-minute walk, grip shoes). 12:00 lunch at a local restaurant in Munnar town (about Rs. 150 per person; Kerala biryani, kappa + meen curry). 13:30 Tea Museum (KDHP). 14:30 Mattupetty Dam, boat on the lake. 15:45 Echo Point. 16:45 Top Station viewpoint. 18:30 Munnar town street food and spice shopping (parotta + beef fry, pazham pori, cardamom chai; about Rs. 150 per person). 19:30 back at Sea Grace Cottage, bonfire if the owner can arrange it, sleep by 22:00 for a 05:30 start. Tonight: ask the owner to arrange 2 jeeps for Kolukkumalai at 06:30.
- **Day 2 (Sat): Munnar → Kolukkumalai → Thekkady.** 05:30 wake up and quick breakfast (appam + stew or puttu, about Rs. 80). 06:30 drive to Suryanelli base (35 km). 07:30 Kolukkumalai tea estate jeep safari (about 2 hrs): the Tempo Traveller cannot go up, so the group transfers to 2 pre-arranged jeeps. 10:00 back at base, checkout, depart for Thekkady via NH85. 13:00 lunch in Kumily (about Rs. 150; parotta + chicken or fish curry) and check in at Ferndale Homestay. 14:30 Sathram jeep safari near the Periyar Tiger Reserve border (about 3 hrs, 2 jeeps). 17:45 quick freshen-up. 18:00 Kalaripayattu show at Kadathanadan Kalari Centre, Kumily (about 2 hrs; pre-book for the group). 20:15 dinner in Kumily (about Rs. 180; prawn curry + appam, fish pollichathu). 21:30 homestay.
- **Day 3 (Sun): Thekkady → Alleppey → Kochi Airport.** 07:00 breakfast near the homestay and checkout (about Rs. 80). 08:00 depart for Alleppey via Changanassery (scenic drop from hills to paddy fields and backwaters). 11:30 Alleppey Beach (45 min; 150-year-old iron pier). 12:30 Alappuzha Lighthouse (45 min; climb 5 floors). 13:30 Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary and boat ride on Vembanad Lake (1.5 hrs). 15:00 lunch and chill at Asado Cafe or Cafe Catamaran in Alleppey (about Rs. 300–500; karimeen pollichathu, filter coffee, banana pancakes). **16:30 depart Alleppey for Kochi Airport.** 18:30 reach the airport (6.5 hours before the 23:04 flight): dinner, charge phones, duty-free (Kerala tea, banana chips, spices, cardamom). 21:00 check-in and security. 23:04 flight to Pune.

## Hard deadlines and warnings (operator-verified)
- **Depart Alleppey by 16:30 without fail:** 83 km to the airport, Sunday traffic, flight at 23:04. Reach the airport by 18:30.
- **Tea Museum (KDHP):** open 09:00–17:00; must be done before 17:00. Rs. 150 per person; about 45 min.
- **Top Station viewpoint:** open 05:00–20:00; Rs. 20 per person, CASH ONLY; 1 km walk from parking.
- **Alappuzha Lighthouse:** open Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–17:30 (Sunday is open); Rs. 20 per person, CASH ONLY.
- **Kolukkumalai:** jeep trail only; a Tempo Traveller cannot go up. 2 jeeps at about Rs. 4,000 each (about Rs. 730 per person for 11). Jeeps may cancel in extreme heavy rain; backup viewpoints near Munnar: Lockhart Gap, or Pothamedu.
- **Sathram jeep safari:** 2 jeeps at about Rs. 2,500–3,000 each; about 3 hrs; wildlife sighting is not guaranteed (about 40% chance of elephants, bison, deer).
- **Kalaripayattu show:** 18:00 slot, Rs. 300 per person; book in advance.
- **Alleppey Beach in the monsoon:** NO SWIMMING; waves are rough and dangerous.
- **Mattupetty Dam:** group boat about Rs. 700 flat (20-seater); speed boat about Rs. 1,100 for 5 people.
- **Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary:** Rs. 50 entry plus Rs. 200 boat; June–August is breeding season for resident birds (migratory birds only November–February).
- If a spot closes for heavy rain or a landslide alert, tell the group leader at once and use the backup viewpoints above.
